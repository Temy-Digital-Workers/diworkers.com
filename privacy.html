Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

1. Who we are

Temy Digital Workers is a trading name of Team Technologies Development Center LLP ("we", "us"), a limited liability partnership registered in England and Wales (OC404423), registered office Ground Floor, 41 Devonshire Street, London W1G 7AJ, United Kingdom. We are the controller of the personal data described in this policy. Contact: hello@diworkers.com.

2. Data we collect

  • Contact and account data: name, business email, company, job title and phone number that you give us when you contact us, sign up or buy a subscription (including through Microsoft Marketplace or a reseller).
  • Subscription and billing data: plan, usage quantities, subscription identifiers and invoices. Payment card details are handled by the marketplace or payment provider, not by us.
  • Customer content: documents and records a digital worker processes on your instructions. For customer content we act as a processor for the customer, under our terms and any data processing agreement.
  • Technical data: logs of sign-ins and worker runs needed to operate, secure and support the service.

3. Why we use it and our legal basis

  • To provide the service and support (performance of a contract).
  • To bill and keep accounting records (legal obligation).
  • To keep the service secure and reliable (legitimate interests).
  • To reply to enquiries and, where you agree, send product news (legitimate interests or consent; you can opt out at any time).

We do not sell personal data and we do not use customer content to train AI models.

4. Who we share it with

Hosting and infrastructure providers, AI model providers acting as our processors, Microsoft or the reseller through which you bought the service, and professional advisers, each under appropriate contracts. We disclose data to authorities only where the law requires it.

5. International transfers

Where data is transferred outside the UK or EEA, we use adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses, including the UK International Data Transfer Addendum.

6. How long we keep it

Account and billing data for as long as the subscription runs and then as long as tax and accounting law requires (normally six years). Customer content and run logs for the period set in the customer's contract, after which they are deleted.

7. Your rights

You may ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your personal data, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. Email hello@diworkers.com. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

8. Cookies

This website sets no cookies and uses no tracking or advertising scripts. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. The service itself uses only the cookies needed to keep you signed in.

9. Changes

We will post any changes on this page and update the date above.
