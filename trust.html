Security and data

Built to be trusted with real systems.

A digital worker touches your books, your clients and your infrastructure. These are the rules every one of them follows.

People decide what matters

Posting, sending and changing things can always require approval. Security response actions are proposals only, every time.

Least privilege

Each worker has its own account in each system, with only the permissions its job needs. Read-only wherever the job allows it.

A full audit trail

Every run records its inputs, the steps it took, the decisions it made, who approved them and what changed.

Your data is not training data

Customer content is used only to do the job. It is never used to train AI models.

Choose where it runs

Our cloud, your own cloud subscription or your own server. For sensitive data, workers can use a private open-source model on your hardware.

We use our own workers on ourselves

Our Compliance Evidence and security workers run on Temy's own infrastructure, so we find our own gaps first.
