Support

Email support@diworkers.com with your company name, the worker concerned and, if you have it, the run ID shown in the worker's history.

Response times

  • First response within one business day (Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 18:00 UK time).
  • A worker that has stopped working for all users is handled first.

Subscriptions bought through Microsoft Marketplace or a reseller

For billing and cancellation, use the channel you bought from. For anything about how a worker behaves, contact us directly.

Security issues

Report suspected vulnerabilities to support@diworkers.com with "Security" in the subject line.
