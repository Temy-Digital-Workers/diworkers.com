Temy Digital Workers

AI workers that do real back-office work inside the systems you already use.

Each digital worker is one job done end to end: it reads the source documents, does the work in your business system, and asks a person to approve before anything is posted or sent. Every step is logged as evidence.

The workers

Built for Microsoft-centred businesses and the IT partners who serve them.

Finance

AP Clerk

Reads supplier invoices, matches them to vendors and purchase orders, and drafts bills in Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central or QuickBooks for approval.

Finance

AR and Collections

Tracks overdue invoices, drafts polite reminders and records promises to pay, so cash comes in without a person chasing every customer.

Compliance

Compliance Evidence Officer

Collects evidence for ISO 27001 and SOC 2 controls from Microsoft 365, Entra, Defender and Purview, grades each control and explains every finding.

Security

Alert Triage

Reviews Microsoft Defender alerts, enriches them with threat intelligence and known-exploited vulnerability data, and proposes the next action for an analyst.

Sales

Sales Assistant

Enriches and qualifies leads in Dynamics 365 Sales or HubSpot and writes the results back to the CRM record.

Microsoft 365

Copilot Readiness

Checks a Microsoft 365 tenant for the permissions, labels and sharing settings that should be fixed before rolling out Copilot.

How it works

Connect

The worker connects to your systems with its own scoped account. You choose what it may read and what it may change.

Work and ask

It does the job and stops for a person's approval before posting, sending or changing anything that matters.

Prove

Every run keeps its inputs, decisions and results, so you can see what was done, by whom and why.

Your data stays yours

Workers can run in our cloud or inside your own Azure subscription. Customer data is used only to do the job and is never used to train models.

Pay for work done

Simple monthly plans, with usage-based options priced per document or per task.
