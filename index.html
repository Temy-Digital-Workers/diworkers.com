    What is a digital worker

    Not a chatbot. A colleague with one job.

    A chatbot answers questions. A digital worker finishes work: it takes the input, does every step in your systems and hands you the result, with the evidence.

    Works in your systems

    It signs in to QuickBooks, HaloPSA, HubSpot or your cloud with its own limited account, and works there like a careful new hire would.

    Asks before it acts

    Approval gates sit wherever you want them. Low confidence, a new supplier or a risky change goes to a person in chat first.

    Shows its work

    Every run keeps its inputs, its decisions and its results. You can always see what was done, why, and who approved it.
