Real case

Case: our own Microsoft 365 tenant

We pointed the Compliance Evidence worker at our own Microsoft 365 tenant and it graded 19 controls mapped to ISO 27001:2022, SOC 2 and Cyber Essentials, using Microsoft Graph and public DNS only. 8 passed, including enforced MFA, blocked legacy sign-in, and SPF, DKIM and DMARC on our domain. 6 failed with 11 specific gaps, each with the evidence behind it and the change that would close it. 5 more were marked not collected rather than passed, because the worker did not yet have permission to read those systems. On our engineering estate (DigitalOcean, GitHub, Jira and TLS certificates) the same worker grades 31 controls.

Questions

Yes. A control is a check plus a rule for grading it.
