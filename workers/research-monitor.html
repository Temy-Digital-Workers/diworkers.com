Real case

Case: four research workers we run every day

AI Impact ranks AI news by its impact on the industry and has scored 793 stories. A legislative digest reads the Ukrainian parliament's news feed daily and publishes only the most valuable items. A business-events worker finds conferences and extracts dates, venues and ticket prices. A places worker rates restaurants against a written rubric. All four publish to their own portals.
