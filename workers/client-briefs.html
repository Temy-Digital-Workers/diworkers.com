Real case

Case: a 100-client IT provider, end to end

We built a realistic IT-provider dataset of 100 fictional clients in HaloPSA and Hudu, with problems planted in it (expiring warranties, failed backups, licence gaps). Client Briefs runs on a schedule, refreshes a client when its data changes, and publishes five briefs per client with links straight back to the ticket or the Hudu record. Passwords stored in the documentation platform are read only as names and dates, never as values.

Questions

What people ask.

Does it change anything in HaloPSA or Hudu?

No. It only reads. Briefs are published to the worker's own portal.

Can we use it with another PSA?

The flow is built from connectors; another PSA or documentation tool means swapping two steps, not rebuilding the worker.
