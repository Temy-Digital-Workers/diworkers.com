Real case

Case: a Business Central test company

We ran the AR Collections Clerk on the CRONUS UK test company in Dynamics 365 Business Central. It read 13 open sales invoices across 4 customers, aged them, and found £44,005.48 overdue, all more than 90 days past due. It wrote one reminder per customer, each with a table of that customer's invoices built from Business Central figures, and left all 4 in Outlook Drafts for a person to review and send. Nothing went to customers; it sent one summary with the ageing table and the cash-in forecast to the finance mailbox.
