Real case

Case: 25 supplier emails into Business Central

We emailed 25 UK supplier documents to an AP mailbox and ran the AP Clerk against a Dynamics 365 Business Central company (the CRONUS UK demo). 17 were entered as draft purchase invoices with the right G/L accounts and the PDF attached, with no human touch. 5 went to a person: a blurry phone photo, a vague "services as agreed" line, an invoice whose lines did not add up, and 2 new suppliers. It stopped a byte-identical resend and a reused invoice number, and recognised a statement as not an invoice. On 7 drafts Business Central's VAT total differed from the printed one because those suppliers were set up without UK VAT, and the worker flagged each before posting. The same worker also runs on QuickBooks Online, where a 25-invoice run posted 18 bills with no human touch.

Questions

What people ask.

Does it post anything without approval?

Only what you allow. You set the confidence threshold and which suppliers or amounts always need a person. Everything else is posted and reported.

What if the invoice is a bad scan?

It reads scans and photos, but if a field cannot be read with confidence it asks rather than guesses.

Can it handle VAT and multiple currencies?

It reads tax lines and currency from the document and uses the tax codes set up in your accounting system.
