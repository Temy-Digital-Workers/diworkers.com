Real case

Case: 25 invoices through a real accounting flow

We ran the AP Clerk on a QuickBooks Online test company with 25 supplier invoices sent through Google Chat and email. 18 were posted as bills with no human touch. 3 went to a person because confidence was below the threshold (82%, 86% and 74%). It caught 1 duplicate before it reached the books, and 1 invoice was rejected by the reviewer. Every invoice keeps its source file, its confidence score and the person who approved it.

Questions

What people ask.

Does it post anything without approval?

Only what you allow. You set the confidence threshold and which suppliers or amounts always need a person. Everything else is posted and reported.

What if the invoice is a bad scan?

It reads scans and photos, but if a field cannot be read with confidence it asks rather than guesses.

Can it handle VAT and multiple currencies?

It reads tax lines and currency from the document and uses the tax codes set up in your accounting system.
