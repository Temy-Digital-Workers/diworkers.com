Real case

Case: our own HubSpot

We run the Sales Assistant on Temy's own HubSpot portal. It processes leads in batches every hour, skips anything already enriched, and adds its results in separate fields so a person can always see what came from the worker. Its hub shows each lead with a link back to the HubSpot record.

No. It researches and records. Outreach stays with your team.
