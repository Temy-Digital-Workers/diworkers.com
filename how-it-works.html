How it works

A worker is a flow: steps you can see, test and trust.

Every digital worker runs on Temy Labs, our platform for durable AI work. A worker is a flow of steps. Each step is a script, an AI agent or a person, and the platform makes sure every step finishes, is recorded and can be replayed.

AP Clerk

The real AP Clerk flow, in its QuickBooks version: a Google Chat message starts it, the hub records the invoice, the AI agent codes it, QuickBooks receives the bill, and the chat gets a report. It loops for up to 30 rounds and parks for a person if something is off.

Flows, not black boxes

Each worker is a graph of named steps. You can read it, change it and see exactly which step a run is in.

Durable by design

Runs survive restarts and outages. A failed step retries or parks for a person; it never silently disappears.

Approval gates

Any step can wait for a person in chat. The run pauses, keeps its place and continues on approval.

Its own identity

Each worker uses its own credentials, injected for one run only and encrypted at rest.

Everything recorded

Logs, reports, artefacts and cost for every run, kept with the run and visible in the worker's portal.

Runs where you need it

In our cloud, in your own cloud subscription or on your own server, with Claude or a private open-source model.

Architecture

How data flows through a worker.

A trigger starts a run. The control plane freezes the flow, its prompts and its identity into the run; an execution worker carries out every step and reports each result back.

Pricing

Pay for work done.

Each worker has a simple monthly plan. Where the volume varies, such as invoices or briefs, there is a usage-based plan priced per document or per task, with a monthly cap so the bill never surprises you.
